01A day here

The day in this house,
written in the order the light arrives.

07:40 Morning

You wake to birds, and a breakfast basket is waiting at the door.

Birds in the Gotjawal instead of an alarm. At 8:30 a breakfast basket is left at the door. Grind the beans from Jocheon Roasters, pour slowly, then sit in the wooden chair in the yard and watch the mist lift.

Breakfast basket
08:30, at the door
Coffee
Jocheon Roasters, 100g at every check-in

13:20 Afternoon

A walk in Dongbaekdongsan, then a nap on the floor when you get back.

The entrance to the Dongbaekdongsan wetland is a twelve-minute walk. The loop takes a little over an hour, which makes it easy to fit around lunch. When you come back the sun is lying long across the living-room floor, and opening a book often turns into a nap.

Dongbaekdongsan entrance
12 min on foot, about 1 hour round
Living-room shelf
About 60 books, half of them about Jeju
Light on the floor
South-facing; deepest between 12:00 and 15:00

19:50 Evening

You fill the outdoor bath and wait for the sky to go dark.

The tub takes about forty minutes to fill. In the meantime you can heat soup on the induction hob, pick a film for the projector, or do nothing at all. Once the village goes dark there are more stars than you would expect.

Outdoor bath
Hinoki, seats two, 60cm deep, until 22:00
Bath salts
Yuja or hinoki — one is set out for you

Please finish with the outdoor bath by 22:00. The neighbours are close, so after that the yard is kept for sitting quietly.

02The house

Four rooms and one yard.
The old farmhouse frame was left as it was.

Living room

24㎡ · south-facing

The ceiling rafters were kept and the floor relaid in oak. A low sofa, a solid-wood table for four, a bookshelf, a projector on the wall. Open the window and the yard is right there.

Bedroom

16㎡ · east-facing

A king bed (180×200) with linen bedding. Morning light reaches this room first, so there are blackout curtains as well. On request we lay out two extra floor mattresses in the living room.

Bathroom

8㎡ · skylight

A shower stall and basin under the skylight. Aromatica amenities, four thick cotton towels, a hairdryer, cotton buds and pads, kept stocked.

Yard

120㎡ · stone wall

A yard enclosed by a Jeju stone wall. A hinoki outdoor bath for two, two wooden chairs, one persimmon tree. In the evening only the low lights stay on. We set up a barbecue grill if you ask in advance.

06House notes

A good place to stay isn't one with a lot to do. It's one where doing nothing is fine.

Kim Seon-yeong · who looks after the house Living in Seonheul-ri since 2023, rebuilding this house.

This was a farmhouse built in 1974. Propping up the failing walls, cleaning the rafters and rebuilding the stone wall around the yard took ten months. Instead of replacing things with new ones, we only added what would last: an oak floor, linen, a hinoki tub. The quiet fills in the rest on its own.

Below are the things we would ask you to keep to. Less a set of rules than how people live alongside each other in this village.

  • Check-in is from 16:00 to 22:00 and is self-service. We text the door code at 14:00 on the day.
  • Please tell us in advance if you will arrive after 22:00. The village lanes are dark, so we will leave the yard lights on.
  • Check-out is 11:00. We only ask that you wash up and sort the rubbish; leave the bedding as it is.
  • Parking is for two cars in front of the yard. Please don't park along the village lane — tractors use it.

  • After 22:00, please keep your voices down in the yard. The older neighbours go to bed early. The outdoor bath closes at the same hour, and please turn the projector and speaker down after 23:00.
  • If anyone beyond your booked party is visiting, even briefly during the day, we'd appreciate a heads-up.

  • The breakfast basket is left at the door at 8:30. If you'd like it later, text us by 21:00 the night before.
  • If you have an egg, dairy or wheat allergy, mention it in your enquiry and we will swap those items out.
  • Coffee is restocked at every check-in. If you run out, there is more on the shelf above the kitchen.

  • Children under four don't count toward the guest number. We set out a bed guard and a high chair.
  • The outdoor bath is 60cm deep, so children should get in with an adult. There are fields beyond the stone wall, so it's safer to keep the low gate shut.
