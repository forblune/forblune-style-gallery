  1. 01

    Thickness

    Max band thickness

    2.6mm

    Thinner than two coins stacked. The inner face is pressed flat so it never catches on the finger beside it.

    The band is grade 5 titanium. The inner coating is a hypoallergenic epoxy resin, and the sensor windows sit within 0.1mm of the band surface.

  2. 02

    Battery

    One charge, continuous readings

    7d

    Charge it Monday morning and it lasts to the next Monday. The case refills the ring six more times.

    About 55 minutes from empty to full. The case is USB-C; its own capacity will be disclosed on launch day.
