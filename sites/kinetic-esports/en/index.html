TeamAbout · history · contact

APEX NINE away from the game screen. Seven years from four PC-bang machines in 2019 to a training house in Mapo-gu — and how to reach us.

A team that started on four PC-bang machines in 2019 now closes games faster than anyone in the league.

APEX NINE is a five-player pro MOBA team with a training house in Mapo-gu, Seoul. From Division 2 in its founding year to promotion in 2021 and a first title in 2025 — six years. Five starters and one sixth man, four coaching staff, nine on operations and content.

The training rule is simple. Eight scrim sets a day, a 15-minute review straight after each, physical training twice a week, and managed sleep. The analysis team refreshes opponents' early routing from their last 30 games every week and feeds it into the draft.
